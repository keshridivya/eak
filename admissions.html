BEFORE YOU APPLY

What families
need to know.

Eligibility

Placement depends on the student's age, previous schooling, available seats and the school's admission policy. Exact criteria should be confirmed with the admissions office.

Documents to prepare

  • Student's birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school report / transfer certificate, where applicable
  • Parent or guardian identification and contact details
  • Any additional documents requested by the school

Important dates

Admissions enquiry window: October 2026 – March 2027
